Tier 1 (required):
  .text.seq_chord_symbol  254 B  (0xfe)
  .rodata.CH_MASK          12 B  (6 x uint16)
  .rodata.CH_SUF           19 B
  TOTAL                   285 B
  at -Oz (same source):   248 B text + 12 + 19 = 279 B

The final-spec HOST ESTIMATE of 90-140 B for Tier 1 is NOT realistic.
Code alone is 254 B at -Os / 248 B at -Oz: the matcher, three put_s
calls, and % 12. 90-140 B is about half of the measured text section.

Tier 2 (optional, seven entries):
  CH_MASK                 +14 B  (7 x uint16)
  CH_SUF                  +32 B
  text                      0 B  (same 254 B; the loop bound is a constant)
  TOTAL incremental        46 B  (~6.6 B per entry)
  The 6-10 B per Tier 2 entry estimate IS realistic (data only).

Vs Elite 248 B flash headroom and the 80-200 B display-fix estimate:
  Tier 1 alone at 285 B already exceeds 248 B.
  Tier 1 + display fix, desk range 365-485 B, is well over Elite.
  Do not stack this on the bounded fix without a flash decision.
  Firmware Main Builder must measure at link. Do not change the
  naming rules to chase the 90-140 B figure.
