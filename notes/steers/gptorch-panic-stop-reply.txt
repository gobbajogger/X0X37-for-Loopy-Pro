I’ll answer both as direct live-use recommendations, including whether STOP holds are detectable in MMC mode, and cite the pinned source lines.

1. PANIC: I recommend FX+SEQ on the keyboard and BANK L+R on the SMC in DAW mode, rather than STOP held for >1 second. Neither held gesture works reliably in stock MMC mode.

Long STOP has one advantage: a single transport button is easier to remember and press under stress. But on this build its disadvantages outweigh that:

Keyboard: there is no separate STOP input. PLAY toggles start/stop, so “hold STOP, short STOP unchanged” cannot be added as described. panel.c:29–37, ui.c:3734–3745.
SMC DAW: the physical PAUSE/STOP-position input already captures target lock immediately and unlocks after 900 ms on a subsequent hold. PANIC at >1 s would overlap that operation. smc_mixer.c:701–727, 1974–1977.
SMC MMC: STOP arrives as a command, without release or a duration. Starting a one-second timer would turn an ordinary short STOP into delayed PANIC. Waiting for release would never fire. BANK L+R has the same limitation if interpreted as a held chord. smc_mixer.c:2080–2085 and 2109–2111.
Even with a real release-capable STOP, preserving immediate short STOP means transport stops before PANIC fires. It also leaves stuck notes sounding for over a second.

The two-button choices separate emergency action from normal transport and can use a shorter deliberate hold. Their tradeoff is harder finger placement, which still needs a physical usability check. FX and SEQ currently navigate on release; consume both releases after PANIC. ui.c:3806–3831, 4203–4210. BANK buttons currently navigate immediately and can recapture locked targets, so chord recognition must suppress those side effects before they happen. smc_mixer.c:1468–1479.

Recommendation for MMC: reject hold-based PANIC altogether unless the preset is changed to provide press/release. Do not infer a hold from silence. The combos are preferable candidates, not already implemented or proven accident-proof.

2. PLAY during a question: cancel as NO, then perform normal PLAY behavior is safer live than keeping the question open.

It removes the pending destructive operation and restores predictable performance control. Keeping it open preserves an old CLEAR/LOAD/SAVE intent while playback and context change; revalidation adds failure paths and still leaves an accidental later YES possible. The cost of cancelling is simply reopening the question if it was intentional.

Use the existing NO path, which restores the prior overlay without running the confirmed action, then dispatch transport once. Currently the question blocks PLAY on both devices. ui.c:3720–3731, smc_mixer.c:1807–1810.

I recommend NO + normal PLAY toggle, not unconditional “force playing.” Start when stopped; stop when running. That preserves the existing keyboard/SMC behavior and lets the performer stop on the beat. ui.c:3734–3745, smc_mixer.c:1947–1955. Preserve HOME+PLAY redo, act only once on a fresh press, and still release previously held notes/FX.
