Intentional behavior that must not be “fixed away”

These follow your decisions; this list does not claim they are all implemented:

PLAY does not open a popup. Rewind/Forward use the top notification bar.
SMC can answer the keyboard’s active yes/no dialog.
Pattern changes wait for the bar boundary. Preserve existing PLAY and undo/redo timing rather than inventing new quantization from the shorthand.
PANIC is long HOME, not an ordinary HOME tap.
A muted part ignores fader movement, then jumps to the physical fader position on unmute. Generic pickup must not override this exception.
Tempo changes immediately.
Autosave is optional and silence-only. STOP, mute or zero output alone must not be treated as proof of safe silence.
Queued mutes are controlled by a setting, not compulsory.
FX throw and 909/808 fill belong on the SMC FX page.
HOME+part restores the saved sound.
Scenes are postponed; 32 patterns is an accepted limit.

Historical SMC behavior to preserve unless explicitly superseded: unlocked page-follow, per-peer locked mappings, and the temporary page peek that returns to the remembered page without unlocking. Unexpected page movement while locked is a bug; the temporary peek itself is not.

Unrun checks stay NOT_RUN.

I would make those explicit acceptance conditions for 0.0.24, while keeping the musical decisions above as protected regression expectations.
