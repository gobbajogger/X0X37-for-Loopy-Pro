Groovebox firmware for the M-VAVE SMK-37 (Pro, Elite, MKE-P37) and the FM-1. A 909, an 808, two 303s with TB-3PO, and a breakbeat player — with an APC40-style clip launcher for Loopy Pro on the SMK-37.

Install any published version from Chrome or Edge over USB. The sample importer puts your own loops into the BREAK slots. Installing is at your own risk; packages keep the keyboard's model name so M-UPGRADE still works.
Install What's new?Upgrade, downgrade, or switch: pick a published version and model, or drop any .fwsc (stock / other custom). Nothing to install on the computer.
Sample importerDrop in loops and send them to the BREAK part from Chrome or Edge. ProjectsSave, load and reset on the keyboard, or export a project with its original samples.
