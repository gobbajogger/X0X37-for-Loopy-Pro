These slots will be rewritten on the device. Listen first if you like. Cancel does not write anything.
A backup includes every slot and its original encoded bytes. Restore reports all skipped slots. Close other MIDI applications; this firmware cannot exclude a second external writer atomically. Download pre-write recovery backup

New loops are stored at 44.1 kHz. A one-bar loop at 120 BPM takes about 86 KB; tick "22 kHz" to fit twice as many at a lower quality. Loops follow the tempo by playing faster or slower, so their pitch follows it too; the break's PITCH knob shifts it. Removing a loop renumbers the ones after it, so patterns that used them play the next loop instead. The manual's BREAK section says how X0X plays a loop.
