How to use this guide · what is and is not simulated

Try the critical paths

  1. On HOME, move SMC fader 1. It waits until it reaches the x0x target. Turn its encoder: pickup resets.
  2. Choose 909 MIX. Select a drum, toggle pad steps, hold Solo. Switch banks while holding it; release still clears the original drum. Bank 9–11 leaves strips 4–8 inactive.
  3. Press KNOB BANK for clip mode. Load example Loopy feedback. Pads send APC note events; turn host response off to see that a press does not update the returned state by itself.
  4. Choose SCALE or CHORD on the Elite. Turn the ON field on and play keys. The monitor shows processed notes. SMC encoders edit the visible stage, not hidden x0x parameters.

Boundaries

This is a source-informed teaching model, not compiled firmware or a Bluetooth test. Browser interactions do not connect to or flash hardware. BLE, USB, host replies and clock are simulated. There is no audio, latency or stability claim. Sound parameter fields outside the implemented mixer are normalized illustrations. ARP settings are inspectable; ARP scheduling, swing, humanization, strum timing, full menu actions, all engine pages and song editing are not emulated. Three physical BLE links are not supported by this candidate and are not presented as working.

SVG positions are recreated from manufacturer photos/manual diagrams, not engineering CAD. Counts and arrangement are verified; dimensional 1:1 manufacturing precision is not claimed. LED bytes follow committed tables, but a monitor cannot reproduce calibrated physical LED appearance.
