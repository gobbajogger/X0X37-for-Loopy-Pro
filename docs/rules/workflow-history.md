# Historical instruction boundaries

The active entrypoints are root `AGENTS.md`, `docs/rules/workflow.md`, the maintained
skills it selects, and `docs/rules/local-proof.md` for evidence selection. Read
`BUILDING.md` for commands and `LICENSING.md` for distribution terms. Runtime
adapters in `skills/classic-thread-build-to-git/` or
`.cursor/skills/loopyx0x37-classic-build/` do not override them.

The following retained material is historical evidence, not current assignments,
launcher policy, ownership transfer or authorization:

- `docs/plans/full-cycle-orchestrator.md` and other dated `docs/plans/` records.
- `docs/audit/`, dated investigation/handoff documents and `docs/scheduler-handoffs/`.
- `evidence/`, `audit/`, `cloud-handoff/` and `local-handoff/` records, prompts and logs.
- Dated device sessions in `BLE_ATTEMPTS.md`, prior PR bodies and old rule revisions.
- The retained `loopyx0x37-classic-build-skill.zip` export archive, which is not an
  active installation source; use the maintained unpacked adapter and current rules.
- The v0.1.47 Cursor export provenance and removed `docs/agent-rules.md` or
  `docs/rules/{core,install,integration,...}.md` pointers in historical records.
- Numbered successor-thread tooling such as `tools/stage_handoff.py` and historical
  stage-specific fixtures. Their schema checks do not activate that retired loop.

Retain hashes, original reports and failed/unrun observations. Do not recursively
rewrite evidence or treat an old successful check as current proof. Historical
denials are not silently revoked: reconcile the exact action and current authority
before any retry. Historical unpublished or local-only stage scope does not impose
a permanent ban on a later authorized stage.

The retired generic merger `tools/merge_project_prs.py` is read-only. It cannot
certify current role authority, exact combined proof or required review state.
Its candidate report must be consumed by the existing assigned integration owner
using current rules and exact remote inputs; it does not start a second coordinator.
